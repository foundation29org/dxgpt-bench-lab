Patient 1: 3.5-year-old male from unrelated Polish parents. Born at 39 weeks gestation with birth weight 3700g (50th-75th centile), length 57cm (97th centile), head circumference 36cm (75th-97th centile), thorax circumference 33cm (25-50th centile), Apgar score 9 at 1 min. Family history unremarkable. Proteinuria, hematuria and glycosuria noted in early neonatal period. UTI at 12 months. At 3 years developed bronchopneumonia with elevated creatinine. Laboratory investigations showed constant proteinuria, glycosuria, hematuria, eGFR (Schwartz) 30.56 ml/min/1.73 m2, elevated serum creatinine, uric acid, urea, total cholesterol, and triglyceride; normal CBC, blood glucose, albumin, thyroid and liver function tests. Renal ultrasound showed increased cortex echogenicity and decreased corticomedullary differentiation. Normal male karyotype (46,XY).
