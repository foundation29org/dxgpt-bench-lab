A 69-year-old male patient presented with lower abdominal pain. Physical examination revealed a flat abdomen without varicose veins, soft abdomen, with a palpable 3 cm×4 cm hard mass in the left lower abdomen, positive lower abdominal tenderness without rebound tenderness. Bowel sounds were normal at 4 times per minute. The patient had a history of cataracts for over one year, denied history of coronary heart disease, diabetes, hypertension, hepatitis, tuberculosis, or other acute or chronic infectious diseases. He denied food or drug allergies, trauma, blood transfusion history, or other surgical history.
