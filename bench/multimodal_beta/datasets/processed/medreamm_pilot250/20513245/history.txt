A 37-year-old woman presented with a 4-month history of right facial pain and a 3-month history of progressive painful swelling beneath the right ear lobe. Physical examination revealed a soft swelling in the anteroinferior auricular area that was smooth, elastic, mobile and painful on pressure, but not fluctuant. The facial nerve function was normal and the cervical lymph node was not palpable. There were no systemic symptoms. The patient was screened for metastases, and no lung or abdomen lesions were detected.
