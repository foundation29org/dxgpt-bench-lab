A 62-year-old man presented with palpitations occurring four times in the last week. He experienced a fast heartbeat and shortness of breath. The symptoms varied in timing and duration, with two episodes following alcohol intake. He denied chest pain or syncope. His medical history included hypertension treated with amlodipine. On physical examination, his blood pressure was 160/90 mm Hg with a regular heart rate of 82 beats/minute. Heart sounds and respiratory examination were normal.
