A male neonate, born at 36+1 weeks gestation via cesarean section due to scarred uterus and fetal heart rate deceleration, weighing 2700g with Apgar scores of 2-8-9. The infant experienced severe asphyxia at birth requiring intubation and chest compressions. After resuscitation, he presented with respiratory distress and difficulty breathing. Following extubation from mechanical ventilation on day 6, the patient developed recurrent inspiratory dyspnea and stridor. The infant had no relevant family history and no history of laryngeal or thoracic surgery.
