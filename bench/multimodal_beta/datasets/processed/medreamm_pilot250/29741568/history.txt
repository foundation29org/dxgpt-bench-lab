60-year-old Japanese woman presented with a black nodule on the right postauricular area. Initial staging after radical resection and sentinel node biopsy was pT3aN0M0 stage IIA. At 19 months post-surgery, imaging detected right jugular lymph node and lung metastases. Seven courses of pembrolizumab treatment later, evaluation showed further lung metastasis and new liver metastasis. Tumor harbored BRAF V600E mutation. Thirteen days after starting dabrafenib/trametinib, patient developed fever and hepatosplenomegaly. Laboratory findings showed pancytopenia, hyperferritinemia, increased liver enzyme levels, and coagulation abnormalities.
