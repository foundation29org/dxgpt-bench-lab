A 66-year-old woman presented to the emergency department with episodes of palpitations, dizziness, and near fainting that had lasted for a few hours. Her medical history included removal of a left atrial myxoma 15 years ago, after which she experienced recurrent atrial tachycardia managed with modified release verapamil 240 mg once daily. She had recently started sotalol 120 mg twice daily in addition to verapamil and underwent direct current cardioversion for persistent atrial tachycardia one week prior to presentation. Initial workup showed normal urea and electrolytes including magnesium. An echocardiogram revealed normal left ventricular systolic function with no recurrence of left atrial myxoma.
