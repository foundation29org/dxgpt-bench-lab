A 64-year-old male patient from Smoljanci, Istria presented with firm, migrating nodules in the brachial region without history of serious disease. He had no travel outside Croatia but spent recreational time in forests and islands near his residence and owned a pet dog. Physical examination revealed a painless, well-demarcated subcutaneous nodule in his right upper arm measuring 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.0 cm. Ultrasonographic findings of the nodule appeared normal. Laboratory tests showed total blood counts, erythrocyte sedimentation rate and C-reactive protein within normal values. Serologic results for trichinosis, toxocariasis, schistosomiasis, distomatosis and cysticercosis were negative.
