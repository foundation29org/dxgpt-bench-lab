This is a 56-year-old woman who underwent radical nephrectomy and lymph-node resection for clear-cell renal-cell carcinoma in February 2012. During subsequent follow-up, a single metastasis to the body of the left mandible was detected. She was referred in October 2012 for resection of the mandibular metastasis and fibular free-flap reconstruction. Two months later, bone scintigraphy revealed good vascularization of the flap, though additional bone metastases to the right humerus and pelvis were detected. In January 2014, the patient presented with visible bone exposure in the area of the graft with inflammation of surrounding tissues. Over days, there was an increase in bone exposure and necrosis without evidence of acute secondary infection.
