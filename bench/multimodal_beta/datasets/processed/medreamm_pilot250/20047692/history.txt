A 25-year-old female in her third trimester of pregnancy presented with paroxysmal headache, palpitations, sweating, and hypertension up to 230/130 for three weeks. Her medical history was negative for previous hypertension, and physical examination was unremarkable. Initial work-up excluded pre-eclampsia. Her urine catecholamines were markedly elevated: Dopamine 607 μg/24 hours (range 65-400), Norepinephrine 978 μg/24 hours (range 15-80), Metanephrine 108 μg/24 hours (range 24-96), Normetanephrine 4067 μg/24 hours (range 75-375), and Vanillyl mandelic acid 13.6 mg/24 hours (range 2-7). Epinephrine level was normal at 0.8 μg/24 hours (range 0.0-20.0). Initial abdominal MRI showed a 28×19 mm left juxta-adrenal mass that was later found to be brown fat.
