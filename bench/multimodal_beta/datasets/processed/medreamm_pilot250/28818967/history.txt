A 65-year-old man with a 40 pack-year smoking history presented with a one-month history of worsening breathlessness and chest discomfort on walking. He came to hospital following a collapse that occurred after self-administering sublingual glyceryl trinitrate to relieve his symptoms. He reported no history of cough or coryza, had not lost weight, and experienced no haemoptysis. Laboratory testing revealed a raised serum D-dimer level.
