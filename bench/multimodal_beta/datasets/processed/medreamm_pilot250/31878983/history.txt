28-year-old male patient admitted on November 27, 2013, complaining of diarrhea (loose stool, three to five times per day) for over 10 years and hematochezia for about 1 month. Had been diagnosed with iron deficiency anemia 5 months after birth, with hemoglobin level remaining 70-80 g/L (normal range 110-150 g/L) for most of the time. On admission, albumin level was 26 g/L (normal range 35-52 g/L). Liver and renal function were otherwise normal. Patient reported recurrent arthralgia in the knee and ankle joints.
