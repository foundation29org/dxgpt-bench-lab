A 31-year-old woman underwent barium enema examination as part of a medical checkup. She had been healthy without specific complaints and had no significant past medical or family history. The patient was 161 cm in height and weighed 44 kg, appeared well, and the physical examination showed no abnormalities. Complete blood cell count and biochemical analysis of the blood for hepatic and renal function, urine analysis, and fecal occult blood test were all within normal limits. The tumor markers for CEA and CA 19-9 were also normal. No lesions were observed on the upper endoscopy and the abdominal ultrasound.
