A man in his 70s with a history of coronary artery disease, hypertension, and mild aortic stenosis presented with altered mental status, acute kidney injury requiring dialysis, and painful, dusky skin discoloration on his distal extremities. Three weeks prior to presentation, he had undergone coronary angiography and coronary artery bypass graft surgery after myocardial infarction. Laboratory examination revealed a white blood cell count of 9100/μL with 22% eosinophils, blood urea nitrogen level of 70 mg/dL (25 mmol/L), and creatinine level of 6.1 mg/dL (539.2 μmol/L). Urinalysis showed 1+ blood and 2+ protein.
