30-year-old Hispanic male with history of heavy NSAID use. Presented with melena and severe abdominal pain for 3 weeks, with similar but milder symptoms over prior 4-5 months. Initial hemoglobin 7.8 g/dL and mean corpuscular volume 78 fL. Later endorsed having a right swollen testicle (initially missed on previous exams). Physical exam revealed nontender enlarged right testicle. Laboratory markers showed: human chorionic gonadotropin 50 mIU/mL (normal <10), α-fetoprotein 1,990 ng/mL (normal <8), lactate dehydrogenase 534 IU/L (range 25-175).
