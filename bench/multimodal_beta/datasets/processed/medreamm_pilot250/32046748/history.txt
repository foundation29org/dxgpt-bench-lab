A 31-year-old Caucasian man with multiple sclerosis who had received 24 natalizumab infusions and was switched to fingolimod due to persistent high anti-JCPyV antibody index (3.61). After 40 days of fingolimod treatment, he developed seizures and visual field defects. Neurological examination revealed lateral nystagmus to the left, homonymous hemianopia, distal tremor and spasm of the upper limbs. He presented with leukopenia (0.83 × 10^9/L) at hospital admission.
