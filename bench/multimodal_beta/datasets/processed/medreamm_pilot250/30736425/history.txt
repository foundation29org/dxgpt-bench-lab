A 19-year-old previously healthy gravida 3 para 2002 female presented at 28 weeks' gestation after emigrating to the U.S. at 25 weeks of gestation. During the 20th week of gestation, she experienced a maculopapular rash, conjunctivitis, fever, and headache. She and her spouse had unprotected intercourse through the first and second trimester. The patient's initial fetal ultrasound with neurosonography revealed cerebral ventriculomegaly (20-25 mm) with dangling choroid, prominent 3rd ventricle, and a widened cavum septum pellucidum. Amniocentesis showed negative NAT for ZIKV with no evidence of chromosomal variations by CMA; TORCH panel was negative.
