A 30-year-old male baker from South India presented with 6 weeks of low-grade fever, fatigue, and progressive polyarthritis affecting large joints of arms and legs, without small joint involvement or diurnal variation. He had no relevant medical history and denied tuberculosis contact or high-risk sexual behavior. Physical examination revealed fever (38.9°C), multiple dull red tender nodules on forehead, left cheek, and sternum, and a solitary 1x1 cm firm right cervical lymph node. Musculoskeletal examination showed active arthritis of knee, hip, and elbow joints bilaterally. The liver was palpable 4 cm below right costal margin (liver span 16 cm). Laboratory findings showed normal full blood count and erythrocyte sedimentation rate (6 mm/h protein (131 mg/L). Tests were negative for antinuclear antibodies, IgM-rheumatoid factor, anti-neutrophil cytoplasmic antibodies, antistreptolysin O, and antiDNase B antibodies. Four blood cultures were sterile, and tests for Gonococci and syphilis were negative. Serum calcium and angiotensin converting enzyme were normal.
