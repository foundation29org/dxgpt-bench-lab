36-year-old female with no past medical history who developed lower limbs and para-spinal muscle spasms that progressed over 3 months. Her symptoms continued to progress and she became wheelchair-bound 6 months later. When reviewed at institution, she was severely disabled requiring regular IVIG treatments at a dose of 90g every 12 days. She was taking regular diazepam at a dose of 30mg per day and morphine up to 60mg a day to control pain. On examination, she had brisk reflexes and severe clonus. The muscle spasms were severe enough to compromise her breathing and she required intermittent oxygen.
