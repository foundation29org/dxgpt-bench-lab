A 2-year-old Korean male was admitted for evaluation and management of left hip pain. Physical examination revealed several palpable lymph nodes in the left inguinal area and an ill-defined heterogeneous mass-like lesion in the left anteromedial muscle. Peripheral blood examination showed hemoglobin 9.6 g/dL, white blood cell count 6.83 × 10^9/L, platelet count 182 × 10^9/L, and 5 atypical lymphocytes per 100 white blood cells. Immunophenotyping of neoplastic cells showed positivity for CD45 (99.0%), CD19 (94.84%), CD10 (27.64%), CD20 (94.24%), HLA-DR (95.70%), surface IgG lambda (96.45%), CD13 (22.46%), and CD117 (22.22%), and negativity for CD34 (0.01%), Tdt (0.64%), myeloperoxidase (0.01%), and various T-cell and myeloid markers.
