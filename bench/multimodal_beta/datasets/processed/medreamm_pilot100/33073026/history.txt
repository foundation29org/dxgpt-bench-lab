7-day-old female newborn born at full-term to a G1P1 mother with birth weight of 4,380 gm. Parents denied poor feeding, poor activity, or weak crying. On examination at Day 7, she had normal muscle power, normal reflex, no macroglossia, but slightly decreased facial folds. Newborn screening for Pompe disease showed GAA activity 0.18 uM/h (critical cutoff <0.5) and ratio 42.87 (critical cutoff acid β-glucosidase/GAA ≥20). Laboratory examination revealed elevated pro-BNP (8,738 pg/mL), CK (722 U/L), and ALT (112 U/L). Confirmatory testing showed deficient lymphocyte GAA activity (1.33 nmol/g pro/hr, normal mean 66.7). Patient did not have the common CRIM-positive Taiwan Pompe disease p.D645E variant. Mutation analysis revealed heterozygous c.2024_2026del (p.N675del) and c.2040+1G>T variants in trans.
