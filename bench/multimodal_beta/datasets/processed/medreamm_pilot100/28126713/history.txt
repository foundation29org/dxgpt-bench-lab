A 60-year-old woman presented after sustaining direct trauma to the anterior neck from a fall. She developed progressively worsening hoarse voice, difficulty swallowing, and pain with neck movement. Physical examination revealed visible neck swelling with bruising tracking onto the anterior chest wall. There was no tenderness on palpation of the cervical spine. On flexible nasal endoscopy, swelling of the posterior pharyngeal wall was observed, indicating potential airway compromise.
