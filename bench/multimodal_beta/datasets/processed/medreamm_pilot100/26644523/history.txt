45-year-old woman presented with syncope and severe anemia (hemoglobin 6 g/dL) with lower GI bleeding. A diagnostic colonoscopy was negative. CT scan of abdomen and pelvis revealed a 3.2 x 3 x 2.9 cm contrast-enhancing right lower-quadrant mass arising from the wall of the ileum. No evidence of metastatic disease. Subsequent laparotomy found a 3.5 cm mass that was resected with negative margins. Molecular studies showed a 42-base pair deletion in exon 11 of the KIT gene (V559_D572del) that would delete all or part of codons 558 to 572 and would change the 558-encoding amino acid from Lys to Asn (K558N).
